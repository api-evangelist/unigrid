# UNIGRID

> UNIGRID (UNIGRID Inc., unigridbattery.com) is a San Diego-based sodium-ion
> battery company founded in 2021 out of UC San Diego Ph.D. research by
> Dr. Darren H. S. Tan and Dr. Erik A. Wu. Its sodium chromium oxide (NCO)
> chemistry and alloy anode technology deliver fire-safe, fully passive
> batteries rated for 30,000+ cycles and -40°C to 60°C operation, sold as 12V
> starter batteries (Na+ Fleet), BMS-less rack modules (Na+ Module),
> residential energy storage (Na+ Casa), and DC rack systems for C&I and data
> centers (Na+ Cleo). Cells are UN38.3 and UL 9540A certified. Backed by
> Transition VC, Ritz Venture Capital, Union Square Ventures, and Foothill
> Ventures ($12M Series A, 2024). UNIGRID publishes no public API or
> developer program.
